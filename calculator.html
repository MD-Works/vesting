Property Purchase Price

Transfer Duty Payable
R0

Based on SARS 2025/26 transfer duty brackets for natural persons (effective 1 April 2025). Transfer duty is paid by the buyer to SARS via the transferring attorney, separately from conveyancing and Deeds Office fees. Companies, CCs and trusts are taxed under the same table. If the seller is a VAT-registered vendor, VAT applies instead of transfer duty. Always confirm the current brackets on sars.gov.za, as rates are reviewed annually.
